# Data availability

The actual-ET study uses public OpenET and processed flux archives:

- https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.10119477
- https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7636781

The source manifest records archive checksums.
The results retain the selected 7,923-row cohort and exact station, proximity-group, and time partitions.
The selective experiment retains inner predictions, outer predictions, fault controls, and matched-budget comparisons.
The source humidity audit keeps the exported values and their documented units.
It does not substitute invented repaired measurements.

The synthetic faults alter test inputs while retaining labels and satellite estimates.
They are paired robustness probes, not additional independent observations.
The unchanged reference-ETo figures retain their source records under `data/outlook/` and `manuscript/arxiv/figures/figure_data.json`.
No private field data or correspondence is required for the ML experiment.
